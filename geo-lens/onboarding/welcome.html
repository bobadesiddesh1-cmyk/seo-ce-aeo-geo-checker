GEO Lens is installed

It shows you which sentences on a page are holding it back from being cited by AI search — and gives you the corrected text to paste in.

Run your first scan

  1. Open any article or blog post.
  2. Click the GEO Lens icon, or press Alt + Shift + G, or right-click the page and choose Scan this page with GEO Lens.
  3. Problems are highlighted in colour, right where they occur, and the side panel explains each one.

What the four colours mean

ExtractabilityCan an engine lift a direct answer out of the prose?
StructureQuestion headings, lists, comparison tables, jump links.
Entity & E-E-A-TSourced claims, first-hand experience, entity clarity.
CitabilityAre individual sentences quotable and confident?

The three panel views

  1. Issues — every problem, with a generated rewrite you can copy.
  2. Quotable — the sentences an engine is most likely to cite verbatim. If none qualify, it says so.
  3. Retrieval — how the page splits into chunks, and which chunks lose their subject when retrieved alone.

Scope

GEO Lens covers the editorial layer only — the prose itself. It deliberately does not check titles, meta descriptions, H1 counts, canonicals, schema validity or crawler access; those are on-page and technical SEO, and a dedicated SEO toolkit handles them better.

Everything runs locally in your browser. No account, no API key, no network requests, nothing leaves the page. Thresholds and report branding are in Settings.
