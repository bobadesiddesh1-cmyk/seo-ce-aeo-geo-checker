GEO Lens

Scan any page for AI-search visibility issues — AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini — with inline colour highlights, a scored AEO/GEO audit, and the corrected text to paste in. 100% local.

What it checks

Extractability

Direct-answer readiness: walls of text, unanswered question headings, buried definitions, missing summaries.

Structure

Question headings, lists vs prose, comparison tables, table of contents.

Entity & E-E-A-T

Sourced claims, first-hand experience, entity consistency in the opening.

Citability

Vague quantifiers, outbound sources, quotable stat-sentences, hedged claims.

It writes the fix

Where a real answer has to be written, Chrome’s built-in on-device model writes it — the direct answer under a question heading, the TL;DR, the opening that names your subject. It runs on your machine: no account, no API key, no network call. Any rewrite that invents a statistic absent from your page is discarded automatically.

Everything else is mechanical — generated replacement text with a Copy button — a wall of text split at real sentence boundaries, a hedge stripped from a claim, a comma run turned into list markup, a comparison table skeleton naming both options. Where a real rewrite would require facts the page doesn’t state, you get a clearly-labelled skeleton, never invented content.

Three more views

Insights surfaces what pattern matching cannot see: a heading whose question is never actually answered, a claim asserted with nothing behind it, and the questions readers arrive with that the page never addresses. Quotable ranks the sentences an engine is most likely to lift verbatim — and says plainly when none qualify. Retrieval shows how the page splits into chunks and flags the orphans: passages that lose their subject once retrieved alone.

Scope

GEO Lens audits the editorial layer only — the prose itself. Titles, meta descriptions, H1 counts, canonicals, schema validity and crawler access are on-page and technical SEO, handled by the companion SEO Sidekick.
